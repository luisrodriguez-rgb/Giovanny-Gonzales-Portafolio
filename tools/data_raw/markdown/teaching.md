I have taught at the Universidad Icesi courses in Econometrics, Econometrics II, Economic Policy, and Macroeconomic Theory I and II in undergraduate program. In masters, I have taught in the MBA, Global MBA, Master in Marketing and Master in Finance the courses of Economic Environment, Managerial Economics, International Economic Policy and Financial Econometrics. At the doctorate level (Ph.D. in Business Economics) I have taught the courses of Quantitative Methods, Time Series and Advanced Econometrics I and II.
