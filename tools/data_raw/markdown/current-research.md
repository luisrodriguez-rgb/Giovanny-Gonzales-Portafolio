Current Research

My areas of specialization are Economics of Education, Economics of Crime, Entrepreneurship and Applied Econometrics. I have published a number of papers in well-known journals. I am a member of the research group

Economics, Public Policies and Quantitative Methods

, Universidad Icesi. Recognized by COLCIENCIAS in the highest category A1 [

GrupLac

] and I am currently classified as an associate researcher [

CvLac

]. Elected by vote to represent professors (Ph.D.) in the Faculty's research committee, 2016-2018. I participate in a research project financed by the Universidad Icesi, about the economic analysis of the demand for higher education in Colombia.

I participate as a referee in several national and international academic journals, I also evaluate research projects of universities and other institutions, I have been a referee of the intellectual production of professors in universities with professor rankings and I have participated in the elaboration of state exams (Saber Pro) for students of economics careers [Icfes (2011 & 2012) and Afadeco (2020)]. I am a member of the

Red de Investigadores de Economía

. Banco de la República de Colombia [Central Bank]. Top 100 IDEAS Repec 10A, 2018.

I supervised Magaly Herrera's doctoral dissertation at the Universidad Icesi (2021) and participated in Gustavo Junca's thesis committee at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia (2019). I am editor-in-chief of the working papers: (1) Icesi Economics Working Papers and (2) Icesi Economics Lecture Notes. Also, I participate in editorial committees of several national academic journals, PRJ. Member of the scientific committee of the Economics of Education Association.
